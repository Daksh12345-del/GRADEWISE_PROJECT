# GradeWallah – One-Time Project Sale Summary

## One-liner
GradeWallah is a ready-made student SaaS dashboard for SGPA / CGPA calculation, semester-wise academic tracking, placement resources, internships, DSA roadmap, and an AI study coach.

## Why this is valuable to a college or institute buyer
- Students get a single platform for grades, resources, placement links, and study planning.
- The institute gets a ready-made product instead of building from scratch.
- The platform can be used as an internal academic + placement-readiness tool.
- It reduces the confusion of managing academics through WhatsApp, PDFs, and random links.

## What makes this sale different
This is not a yearly license or rental model. The buyer gets a **full one-time transfer**, including:
- Full source code
- Domain name `gradewallah.com`
- Deployment handoff
- Setup and transfer documentation
- Buyer-led maintenance going forward

## What is included in the sale
- Branded domain `gradewallah.com`
- Full React + Vite frontend
- CGPA / SGPA calculation engine with back-paper logic
- Dashboard and semester tracker
- Placement and internship modules
- DSA roadmap and AI coach features
- Handoff documentation and setup guide

## Pricing guidance
Realistic pricing depends on traction, traffic, users, and revenue.

- **Early stage / low traction:** around Rs 50,000 to Rs 1,50,000
- **Decent traction / active users:** around Rs 1,50,000 to Rs 3,00,000
- **Proven revenue:** usually sold at 2x to 4x annual revenue

Since the buyer wants a one-time transfer, the value increases when the demo is strong, the landing page looks trustworthy, and the technical handoff is clean.

## How to sell this to a college or institute
Do not sell code. Sell the outcome.

1. Show that students get one platform for grades, resources, and placement readiness.
2. Explain that the institute gets a ready-made product without a long development cycle.
3. Offer clean handover with documentation so the buyer can manage updates and fixes.
4. Keep the discussion focused on ownership transfer and business use, not licensing.

## Best buyer targets
1. Coaching institutes with existing student reach
2. Small engineering colleges without their own strong portal
3. Alumni or faculty-led teams wanting a student platform
4. Edtech founders looking for a ready-made product

## Bottom line
Yes, this project can sell. To improve price and speed of sale:
- Keep the live demo strong
- Add a simple landing page with social proof / testimonials
- Prepare a clean technical handoff kit so the buyer does not fear maintenance

A good handoff kit alone increases buyer confidence a lot.
