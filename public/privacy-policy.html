🔍 Part A — What We Collect
§ 01

Information You Provide

When you set up your Gradewallah profile, we ask for a small set of details to personalise your experience:

  • Name — used to greet you and personalise your dashboard.
  • Email address — used for account identification and sign-in.
  • University, college, course, and branch — used to show you the correct grading scheme, study resources, and eligibility criteria relevant to your institution.
Note: As stated in-app, your basic profile info stays on your device unless you choose to sign in, in which case your account is created and authenticated via Clerk, and your profile data is then securely synced via Supabase (see § 09).
§ 02

Account Sign-In

Gradewallah's login is fully passwordless and is powered by Clerk. There is no password field anywhere in the app — every sign-up and sign-in goes through a 6-digit verification code sent to your email address.

You can also choose to sign in using your Google or GitHub account. When you choose this option, we receive only the basic profile information made available by that provider — typically your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not request access to your contacts, files, repositories, or any other data beyond what is needed to identify your account.

We never receive or store a password for your Google or GitHub account, nor do we store a password for your Gradewallah account. Authentication is handled entirely by Clerk and, for OAuth sign-in, the respective provider.

§ 03

Academic & Performance Data

The CGPA Tracker, Grade Calculator, and Result Analyser features require you to enter academic marks, subject credits, and semester details. This data is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate your live SGPA and CGPA.
  • Generate performance insights, charts, and the Academic Journey timeline.
  • Identify weak or borderline subjects through the Smart Analyser.
  • Display a personalised CGPA eligibility indicator on the Placements page.

This academic data is used solely for in-app calculations and is never shared with any employer, recruiter, institution, or third party.

§ 04

DSA Tracker Data

If you choose to use the DSA Tracker, you may provide your public usernames or profile links for platforms such as LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef, and GitHub. Gradewallah fetches publicly available statistics from these platforms (such as problems solved or contest ratings) to display in your dashboard. We do not access any private data on these platforms and do not store your credentials for them.

§ 05

Automatically Collected Data

Like most web platforms, Gradewallah's servers and infrastructure providers may automatically log limited technical information, such as browser type, device type, and general usage patterns (for example, which features are opened). This information is used only in aggregate to improve performance and reliability, and is not used to individually identify or track you across other sites.

⚙️ Part B — How We Use It
§ 06

Purpose of Processing

We use the information described above strictly to:

  • Operate core features — CGPA Tracker, Study Resources, Internship Explorer, Placements, Analyser, and DSA Tracker.
  • Personalise your dashboard based on your university, course, branch, and domain of interest.
  • Match you with relevant internship and placement listings.
  • Maintain and secure your account through Supabase Auth.
  • Respond to support requests sent to our contact email.
§ 07

Third-Party Data Sources

As described in our Terms & Conditions, Gradewallah displays internship and job listings retrieved through official APIs and feeds — including JSearch, Adzuna, the Internshala RSS feed, Remotive, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, and TheMuse. When you click "Apply Now" on a listing, you are redirected to the original third-party site, which has its own separate privacy policy governing any information you submit there. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any external site before applying.

§ 08

What We Never Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to advertisers, recruiters, or any third party.
  • We do not share your academic marks, CGPA, or eligibility data with employers or institutions.
  • We do not use your data to display third-party advertisements within the app.
  • We do not request or store passwords for any third-party platform (Google, GitHub, LeetCode, etc.).
🛡️ Part C — Storage, Rights & Legal
§ 09

Data Storage & Security

Authentication (your sign-in identity, email verification, and OAuth connections) is managed by Clerk. Your profile and academic data synced through sign-in is separately stored in our Supabase database, which provides encrypted data storage and industry-standard security practices. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

§ 10

Data Retention

We retain your account and academic data for as long as your account remains active, or until you request deletion. If you stop using Gradewallah without deleting your account, your data may remain stored until a deletion request is made.

§ 11

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated profile information.
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features such as DSA Tracker integrations at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us using the contact details in § 16. We aim to respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.

§ 12

Children's Privacy

Gradewallah is intended for students of undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

§ 13

Cookies & Local Storage

Gradewallah may use browser local storage or cookies to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as your selected university or dark mode setting. These are used for functionality only and not for cross-site advertising or tracking.

§ 14

Changes to This Policy

Gradewallah reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

§ 15

Governing Law

Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts located in India.
